Use case: illustration-story.
Create a finished illustrated interaction-design storyboard for a university Raspberry Pi project, ready to put in a GitHub README. Portrait canvas with exactly 8 clear equal panels, 2 columns by 4 rows, read left to right and top to bottom. Title "PIPI CLOCK", subtitle "A cheeky talking study buddy". Clean warm white background, fine charcoal pen sketch lines, soft butter-yellow and sky-blue watercolor accents. Charming industrial-design notebook drawing, readable handwritten-style English text.
Same casually dressed university student in every panel. Same tiny desktop device throughout: a small Raspberry Pi with a 1.14-inch landscape color screen, two physical buttons labeled A and B, a small proximity sensor on the front, a webcam with microphone on top, and a separate small USB speaker. The screen shows crisp simple pixel art only: a round yellow egg-shaped pixel pet with two small cat-like ears, a dark brown outline, pink cheeks and tiny orange feet, standing on a floor line under a blue sky. It is an original character, not a copy of any existing brand. No phone app, no unrelated logos.
Story sequence, headings, dialogue and captions:
1 heading "1. Wake". Student waves a hand close to the sensor. On screen the pet jumps with a small heart and a green "LISTENING" label. Pet bubble "Hehe, that tickles! I'm listening." Caption "A hand near the sensor is a head pat."
2 heading "2. Add a task". Student speaks toward the webcam microphone. Student bubble "Remind me to finish Lab 3 at four PM." Screen shows an orange "THINKING" label. Caption "0.8 s of silence ends the turn."
3 heading "3. Confirm". Pet bubble "Finish Lab 3 at four PM. Save it?" Student bubble "Yes." Caption "Nothing is saved until the student says yes."
4 heading "4. Study together". Student bubble "Start a 25-minute timer." Pet bubble "Starting now. I'll be quiet. Mostly." Screen closeup: the pet wears small round glasses and sits at a tiny pixel desk with an open book and a desk lamp, countdown "24:58". Caption "The pet studies with you and stays silent."
5 heading "5. Check the tree". Closeup of a finger pressing the B button; the screen switches to a dark pixel garden with a growing sapling and a countdown. Caption "B switches between the pet and the growing tree."
6 heading "6. Due soon". Screen shows the pet pacing with a sweat drop. Pet bubble "Lab 3 is due in five minutes. Done, or still working?" Caption "It waits up to 8 s for an answer."
7 heading "7. Late!". Student is away from the desk. Screen shows the pet stomping with small red marks and one small brown pixel poop on the floor. Pet bubble "It's late! It's LATE! Do something!" Caption "A missed deadline leaves a poop."
8 heading "8. Done". Student bubble "Done!" Pet bubble "Yay! I'm cleaning up the poop." Screen shows the happy pet with a heart and a clean floor. Caption "The task is ticked off."
Footer below all panels, legible: "Timing: 0.8 s of silence ends a turn; the pet waits up to 8 s for a reply."
Ensure all eight numbered panels and their captions fit completely with wide margins. Speech bubble tails must point to the correct speaker. All text English, exact spelling, no duplicated or missing panels. These are concept illustrations, not a claim of a finished product.
